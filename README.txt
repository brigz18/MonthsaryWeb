OUR LITTLE LOVE STORY - how to open it on Windows
1. Right-click the zip > Extract All (keep the folder together).
2. Open the folder and double-click index.html. It opens in your browser and works offline.
3. Add your song as music/our-song.mp3 (optional, plays only when she taps the Music button).
4. Edit captions and reasons in js/script.js, and timeline text in index.html (open with Notepad).
Photos are images/photo1.jpg - photo27.jpg. Replace a file, keep the same name.
